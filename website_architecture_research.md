# Website Architecture Research

## Core conclusion

The main conclusion is simple: do not copy Vladimir’s number of products or pages. Copy the commercial architecture underneath them.

His ecosystem has an attention layer, a low cost purchase, a recurring knowledge product, and a separate company path. Your version should be much smaller because your current proof base and offer library are smaller.

Your attached source file supports AI strategy, Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, AI agents, solopreneur scaling, systems thinking, energy management, and your EQ × AI positioning. It does not contain your current prices, client results, revenue results, client names, or numeric B2B case studies. So any missing proof should stay empty until it is supported by a source file.

## 1. What Vladimir’s architecture actually does

### aiprofit.rs

Commercial function: B2C acquisition and ecosystem hub.

It takes a broad audience interested in AI and turns them into buyers or members. The current public ecosystem leads into AI Profit Lab, starter products and other education products.

The important lesson is that the main brand does not force one expensive purchase. There are several depths of commitment.

Visitor → learner → buyer → recurring member.

### AI Profit Lab

Commercial function: recurring revenue and retention.

It acts as the deeper paid environment containing AI resources, education, systems and community.

This is where content becomes recurring revenue.

### Claude Turbo Starter

Commercial function: first transaction.

Its strategic role is a narrow, outcome based starter product that asks for a much smaller commitment than membership.

That matters more than copying the exact format.

### firme.aiprofit.rs

Commercial function: B2B qualification.

Company traffic is separated from consumer education traffic and sent toward a commercial conversation.

That is exactly the role your Companies path needs.

### Other products

Commercial function: monetise different stages of awareness.

The mechanism is:

Content → small purchase → larger relationship → company work.

That is the piece worth copying.

## 4. One site or B2B subdomain

Recommendation: keep one site now.

Do not launch a separate B2B subdomain yet.

One domain gives you:

one pool of authority

one Google footprint

one LinkedIn destination

one analytics setup

The split becomes worth it when the B2B business can stand alone commercially.

Recommended threshold:

```text
3 publishable company case studies with numeric outcomes

2 distinct company offers

30 qualified company leads within 90 days
```

At that point, the company buyer has enough unique proof, offers and traffic to justify its own architecture.

Before that point, a subdomain adds fragmentation.

## 5. Payment path

### Serbian company

Recommended flow:

```text
/companies

→ 3 questions

→ call

→ proposal

→ signed acceptance or contract

→ invoice

→ bank transfer

→ delivery
```

For Serbian B2B work, keep payment off the website initially.

A company buyer does not need a Buy Now button for consulting or implementation.

Use a Serbian business account and invoice the company.

Where applicable, use SEF according to your legal form and the client’s status.

Your accountant should confirm the exact invoicing treatment.

### Individual abroad buying tutoring

Flow:

```text
Website → Preply
```

Do not build another checkout.

Preply already handles the student payment flow.

### Individual abroad buying the 29 EUR product

Recommended platform: Lemon Squeezy.

Reason:

It supports Serbia for payouts.

It acts as merchant of record.

It handles payment processing, VAT or sales tax handling, refunds and chargebacks.

Recommended flow:

```text
Website

→ UZMI AI WORKFLOW STARTER

→ Lemon Squeezy checkout

→ card payment

→ automatic file delivery

→ payout to your business account

→ accounting records
```

This fixes the current payment gap and creates a sale path that works without your calendar.
