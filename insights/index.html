You cannot delegate what you do not understand

You cannot delegate a process you do not understand deeply enough to see every small dependency inside it. I moved 4,500 tasks and dependencies out of scattered spreadsheets. Ten person agency. The mapping took longer than the build. Founders hand over the part they can describe. They keep the part they carry in their head. That second part is the one that breaks.
