Privatnost

Poslednja izmena: 17. septembar 2026.

Šta se prikuplja

Sajt sam ne čuva vaše podatke. Kad odgovorite na tri pitanja na strani za firme, odgovori se ne šalju na ovaj sajt. Prenose se u kalendar za zakazivanje kao napomena uz termin.

Kad zakažete razgovor, Calendly čuva ime, mejl, izabrani termin i tu napomenu. Kad kliknete na časove, prelazite na Preply, koji ima svoja pravila privatnosti.

Ko obrađuje podatke

  • Calendly, za zakazivanje razgovora
  • Preply, za časove jedan na jedan
  • Cloudflare, koji isporučuje sajt

Za šta se koriste

Samo za pripremu i vođenje razgovora koji ste zakazali. Podaci se ne prodaju i ne dele za reklame.

Vaša prava

Možete tražiti uvid, ispravku ili brisanje svojih podataka. Pišite na iva.kojic@icloud.com.

Privacy

Last updated: 17 September 2026.

What is collected

This site does not store your data. When you answer the three questions on the companies page, your answers are not sent to this site. They are passed to the booking calendar as a note on your booking.

When you book a call, Calendly stores your name, email, the chosen time and that note. When you click through to lessons, you go to Preply, which has its own privacy policy.

Who processes it

  • Calendly, for booking calls
  • Preply, for one to one lessons
  • Cloudflare, which delivers the site

What it is used for

Only to prepare and run the call you booked. Data is not sold or shared for advertising.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data. Write to iva.kojic@icloud.com.
