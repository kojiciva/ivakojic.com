Week one · warm network first

23 Junebrief in

Brief received. I sent back two clarifying questions the same day. Whether the target had to sit inside a large corporate or could be an AI native company at enterprise scale, and whether to approach on behalf of The Recursive or as myself.

I wanted the scope fixed before starting.

24 June8 channels

Eight channels opened in a single day

GIZ SerbiaA personal connection into the German development agency. Full corporate brief written from scratch, explaining The Recursive's three divisions and exactly what profile I needed.
LMtek, enterprise hardwareApproached in Serbian. He works the UAE market through distributors.
SP Jain School of Global ManagementEmail plus LinkedIn. The address bounced, domain not found.
Siemens AustriaDirect ask for an internal introduction to their AI side.
weXelerateA former colleague suggested ProSiebenSat.1. Wrong sector. I clarified the brief and let it go.
25 Junethe anchor

Dr Marwan A., CEO of AI at Dubai Department of Economy and Tourism. LinkedIn message at 09:50.

Not a cold approach. We met in 2023 after his talk at the Tomorrow Conference in Belgrade, and he answered warmly at the time.

When LinkedIn went quiet, I asked the friend who had moderated that conference whether he could reach him. He gave me Marwan's private number, on one condition: that I never say where it came from.

I did not say then, and I am not saying here.

26 Junestatus

You confirmed Marwan was a good choice and advised framing it as a chance for him to present Dubai's AI ecosystem to European investors, relationship first.

The LMtek contact came back. He could not help in the UAE, so he personally wrote to the owner of a German company on my behalf.

I also sent you a status update:

I have already reached out to Marwan on LinkedIn. I am holding off on pushing harder for now, since my contact mentioned he has been quite low key lately and I do not want to overstep. My plan: if I do not hear back by Monday, I will send a short informal note on WhatsApp, and if it is still quiet, I will not push further. In parallel, I am working a few other angles so we always have options.
29 Junethe decline

Marwan still silent. I escalated by channel. LinkedIn, then a phone call, then a short WhatsApp message. In the Gulf a voice builds trust faster than another written message.

The GIZ chain, four people deep

My GIZ contact called me rather than replying. His read was direct: the first email was good, but he could not forward it internally because it came from a private domain, and I would know how that looks. So he did the next best thing and went looking for someone in Germany instead.

He passed me to Nikola B., a business consultant at SBP Management in Berlin. I connected with him on LinkedIn, and he in turn put the brief in front of a CEO.

Four people, three days, from a personal connection in Belgrade to a founder's inbox in Berlin.

A CEO said yes, and I turned it down in 44 minutes

Julian S., Geschäftsführer and CEO of Fuxam GmbH, Berlin

At 15:05 he wrote:

I would be happy to help you with your inquiry and would be pleased to agree to an interview. Please send me all the necessary information again and suggest a date and time.

A confirmed CEO interview, four days before the original deadline, with nothing else landed yet.

At 15:49 I declined it. The company is growth stage, and the brief said a serious enterprise. Taking it would have filled the deliverable and missed the point.

To be transparent: for this particular piece I am focused on leaders within larger enterprises, so I think there may have been a small misunderstanding. Fuxam would not be the right fit for this specific format at the moment. That said, I would genuinely love to stay in touch. I think you would be a fantastic fit for our coverage of high growth startups, especially seed stage companies and the investment landscape around them.

He replied eight minutes later: "No problem, anytime!"

I went back to GIZ afterwards and asked them to keep looking. The answer was honest: at enterprise level, in that window, it would be hard.

Week two · the warm side collapses

30 Junethree losses
  • The German route closed. The owner my contact had written to was on a cruise and unreachable.
  • GIZ closed. My contact told me it was the wrong moment to ask, for PR reasons on their side. I dropped the route.
  • Marwan silent across three channels. I stopped. A fourth attempt on a low profile Gulf executive damages the relationship.

I sent the LMtek contact a warm thank you anyway, because he had spent real effort on something that did not work out.

Decision the same day. The warm network was out of moves. Rather than keep working dead threads, I switched to building a cold list properly.

The funnel

  • I paid for CRM access and pulled a filtered export of 50 qualified leads. Filters: Chief AI Officer, CDO, CIO, CTO, Head of AI, at companies over 500 people, in Germany or the UAE, across finance, automotive, defence, energy, retail, telecoms and cybersecurity.
  • 50 became 17. I read them individually and kept the ones who own AI transformation.
  • 17 became 5 for the first send.
1 Julyheaviest day

Eleven formal emails

Apollo used to verify ten enterprise addresses. Subject line "The Recursive, an interview invitation". Formal European register, first name opening, hyperlinked rather than raw URLs, one fully rewritten for a contact I already knew.

Then the task turned. Nikola J., ex McKinsey, working between Dubai and Belgrade, answered my email the same day with two leads. One was Carlo N., Chief Digital Officer at Emirates Global Aluminium. First CDO in the company's history, twenty years at Airbus before that, which makes him a Europe to Gulf bridge rather than only a Gulf contact.

2 Julyfirst yes

Naveen K., E.ON Digital Technology, on LinkedIn:

In principle yes. But would like to understand further, maybe we get on a short call.

Hamed A., Chief AI Officer at the Central Bank of the UAE, accepted the connection the same day.

You asked whether I needed more time. I said yes, a few days would help because two leads were warming. Deadline moved from 3 to 10 July. I asked in advance.

3 Julytwo tracks

Full pitch sent to Hamed Areidat. Second message to Nikola J., extending his window without pressure, asking for either a written yes from Carlo or permission to write to Carlo myself. Two tracks in parallel.

Naveen was confirmed but not C-level, Carlo and Hamed were pending, so I waited until there was something settled to report.

Week three · the anchor and the correction

5 to 6 Julyanchor lands

Carlo's one pager written, then rewritten with a How I reached him section added, because the brief tested sourcing and not just naming.

I also prepared a fallback package in advance, to be used only if both Carlo and Hamed stayed silent.

6 July, Nikola J. came back: Carlo is open to the interview. A C-level yes at a sovereign backed industrial group, sourced through a warm intro opened five days earlier.

7 to 10 Julydelivery

The direct email to Carlo bounced. EGA blocks private domains as corporate security policy. I forwarded it to you the same day with the context.

On 9 July I approached him again on his direct address with you copied in.

Deliverable submitted 10 July.

16 Julyit worked

Marwan came back, three weeks after my last message.

Hi Iva, Email me on marwan@dubaidet.ae

The relationship first approach worked. Just outside the deadline.

21 Julyhanded over

What I handed over

At the leadership call I gave you Marwan Alzarouni directly, and suggested you write to him at Dubai DET using my name, because a business domain gets through where a private one may not.

I did that before knowing the outcome of the process.
